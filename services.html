Services

Everything your firm's finances need

Start with clean books, add trust compliance, and grow into strategy. Four service lines, each built specifically for how law firms bill, hold client funds, and earn revenue.

Advisory

Turn clean books into better decisions

Bookkeeping tells you what happened. Advisory tells you what to do next: where your profit actually comes from, what to charge, and how to plan the year ahead with confidence.

  • Profitability analysis by practice area, matter, and attorney
  • Pricing, billing-rate, and realization reviews — the gap between what you bill and what you collect
  • Annual budgets and rolling cash-flow forecasts
  • KPI dashboards tracking the handful of numbers that drive a practice
  • Partner compensation and distribution planning
  • Return-on-marketing and growth-investment analysis
Bookkeeping

Clean, current books — every month

The foundation everything else is built on. We categorize your transactions, reconcile your accounts, and deliver monthly financial statements on a chart of accounts designed for legal practices.

  • Monthly transaction categorization and coding
  • Bank and credit card reconciliation
  • Legal-specific chart of accounts — retainers, advanced client costs, and earned fees recorded correctly
  • Monthly profit & loss, balance sheet, and cash reports
  • Accounts payable and receivable support
  • Catch-up and clean-up projects for books that have fallen behind
Accounting

Trust compliance and a disciplined close

Client trust accounts are the highest-risk part of a law firm's finances. We reconcile them three ways every month and keep the documentation your state bar expects to see.

  • Monthly three-way reconciliation of IOLTA and client trust accounts — bank statement, trust ledger, and individual client ledgers matched to the penny
  • Individual client ledger maintenance for every matter with funds in trust
  • Compliance documentation and audit-ready records for bar examinations
  • Structured month-end close with controller-level review
  • Payroll coordination and year-end 1099 preparation
  • Tax-ready financial packages delivered to your CPA
Fractional CFO

Executive financial leadership, part-time price

A senior finance professional at the table for your biggest decisions — without the cost of a full-time hire. Ideal for firms that have outgrown bookkeeping-only support.

  • Multi-year financial projections and strategic planning
  • Hiring, compensation, and capacity modeling — know before you grow
  • Debt, banking, and line-of-credit guidance with bank-ready financials
  • Quarterly business reviews with the partners
  • Succession, merger, and practice-sale readiness
  • A single accountable owner for your firm's entire financial picture

Getting Started

How an engagement begins

1 · Consultation

A no-obligation call about your firm: billing model, software, trust accounts, and where the pain is today.

2 · Diagnostic review

We examine your books and trust accounts, flag risks and gaps, and quote a flat monthly fee based on your firm's size and volume.

3 · Clean-up

If the books are behind, a scoped catch-up project brings everything current and accurate before monthly service begins.

4 · Monthly rhythm

Transactions coded, accounts reconciled, trust accounts tied out three ways, statements delivered — every month, on schedule.
