Services

Everything your firm's finances need

Trust compliance, clean books, and the insight to act on them. Three service lines, each built specifically for how law firms bill, hold client funds, and earn revenue.

Trust Compliance

We deliver a bar-compliant three-way reconciliation for all of your trust accounts, so you can focus on client service.

  • Monthly three-way IOLTA / trust reconciliation
  • Bank statement, trust ledger, and client ledgers matched to the penny
  • Individual client ledger maintenance for every matter
  • Bar-audit-ready compliance documentation
  • Retainers and advanced costs recorded correctly
  • Reporting shaped to your state bar's requirements

Bookkeeping and Accounting

Monthly transaction categorization, bank and credit card reconciliation, and bank-ready financial statements built on a legal-specific chart of accounts.

  • Monthly transaction categorization and coding
  • Bank and credit card reconciliation
  • Legal-specific chart of accounts built around matters
  • Bank-ready profit & loss, balance sheet, and cash reports
  • Structured month-end close with controller-level review
  • Payroll coordination and year-end 1099s
  • Catch-up and clean-up for books that have fallen behind

Advisory and Fractional CFO

Big decisions shouldn't feel like guesswork. From daily hurdles to your next growth move, our Advisory Team gives you the clarity, numbers, and strategy to move forward with confidence — and Fractional CFO service brings senior leadership at a part-time price.

  • Profitability by practice area, matter, and attorney
  • Pricing, billing-rate, and realization reviews
  • Annual budgets, cash-flow forecasts, and KPI dashboards
  • Partner compensation and distribution planning
  • Hiring, compensation, and capacity modeling
  • Debt, banking, and line-of-credit guidance
  • Succession, merger, and practice-sale readiness

Getting Started

How an engagement begins

1 · Consultation

A no-obligation call about your firm: billing model, software, trust accounts, and where the pain is today.

2 · Diagnostic review

We examine your books and trust accounts, flag risks and gaps, and quote a flat monthly fee based on your firm's size and volume.

3 · Clean-up

If the books are behind, a scoped catch-up project brings everything current and accurate before monthly service begins.

4 · Monthly rhythm

Transactions coded, accounts reconciled, trust accounts tied out three ways, statements delivered — every month, on schedule.
