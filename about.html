About Us

The accounting firm behind well-run law practices

Attorneys are trained to practice law, not to reconcile trust accounts or read financial statements. ICProfit exists to close that gap — we handle the financial side of your firm so you can focus on your clients.

Our Story

Built for law firms. Only law firms.

Running a law practice means running a business — but law school doesn't cover accounting, trust reconciliation, or financial management. Most firm owners learn it on the fly, usually at the cost of evenings, weekends, and avoidable risk.

ICProfit was founded to take that work off attorneys' plates entirely. Because we serve one industry, everything we do is purpose-built for it: a legal-specific chart of accounts, trust accounting workflows designed around state bar requirements, and reporting that speaks in matters, retainers, and realization — not generic small-business categories.

Beyond clean books, our goal is understanding: which practice areas make money, what to charge, when to hire, and how to grow. That is where accounting stops being overhead and starts being an advantage.

The Team

The people behind your numbers

Ivy Cohen, CPA

Founder & Fractional CFO

Ivy is the founder of ICProfit and serves as Profitability and Growth Advisor and Fractional CFO for the firm's clients. She is a Certified Public Accountant licensed in Florida and New York, with a master's degree in accounting from the University of Florida.

Before starting ICProfit, Ivy spent more than six years at PricewaterhouseCoopers and more than twelve years running finance and accounting departments for businesses with revenues from $16 million to $150 million. For the past six years, she has provided CFO advice to businesses in the $5 million to $40 million range, including law firms.

JH

Jack Healy

Director of Accounting

Over a five-decade career, Jack has established himself as a versatile finance and accounting leader across manufacturing, consumer goods, luxury brands, and distribution. He laid a strong foundation in cost and plant accounting at companies like Johnson & Johnson and Phelps Dodge before moving into senior financial analysis and executive leadership roles with industry giants including International Playtex, Ciba Geigy, and Revlon. His executive trajectory further expanded through pivotal finance positions at McKinnon Enterprises and several premier LVMH brands—Christian Dior, Givenchy, and Guerlain.

Building on this momentum, Jack went on to drive corporate reporting and financial strategy as VP of Finance at Magenta Research, alongside key controllership roles at Victoria’s Secret, Altman Lighting, HALO Branded Solutions, and Norbert E. Mitchell Co., a $160 million petroleum distributor. Today, as Manager and Consultant at ICProfit, he leverages this rich, multi-industry experience to help companies optimize their financial operations, boost profitability, and position themselves for long-term growth.

HC

Harold Codaste

Senior Accountant

Harold is a Senior Accountant at ICProfit with over 14 years of experience delivering full-cycle accounting, bookkeeping, audit support, and financial reporting for professional services firms and small businesses. He holds an MBA as well as bachelor's degrees in Accountancy and Management Accounting. Throughout his career, Harold has expertly managed financial statement preparation, month-end closes, bank reconciliations, and payroll workflows, while leveraging deep platform expertise in QuickBooks, Xero, Gusto, and various property management systems.

Before joining ICProfit, Harold served as the Asia-Pacific Regional Accounting Coordinator at Lexmark, worked as an Audit Supervisor, and taught accounting at the college level. A strong team leader, he excels at streamlining processes, building custom financial workflows, and training bookkeeping staff to maximize operational efficiency and maintain rigorous reporting standards.

How We Work

Three commitments we make to every firm

Compliance first

Trust accounting is where attorneys carry the most risk. Three-way reconciliations happen every month, documented and aligned to your state bar's requirements — no exceptions.

Clarity over jargon

Financial statements only matter if you understand them. We explain your numbers in plain English and point to the specific levers that move profit.

A real partnership

We coordinate with your CPA at tax time, your practice management software every month, and your questions the same business day. One team, fully in your corner.
