Privacy Policy

ICProfit Reconciliation Assistant · Effective October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the ICProfit Reconciliation Assistant (the “App”), operated by ICProfit, handles information. The App is used only by ICProfit staff to provide accounting services.

Information the App accesses

  • QuickBooks Online data, read-only: bank account names and the last four digits of account numbers, and transaction details for those accounts (dates, amounts, payees, memos, check numbers, cleared status).
  • Bank statements provided by clients for reconciliation.
  • Connection tokens issued by Intuit so the App can read QuickBooks data.

The App never writes to, changes, or deletes QuickBooks data.

How we use it

Only to prepare bank reconciliations and explain differences for ICProfit’s clients. We do not sell, rent, or share this information for advertising or marketing, and we do not use it for any other purpose.

How we protect it

  • QuickBooks connection tokens are stored encrypted and are never shown or logged.
  • Only the last four digits of bank account numbers are kept.
  • Client data and reconciliation outputs are kept on ICProfit-controlled systems and are excluded from source code repositories.
  • Access is limited to authorized ICProfit staff.

Service providers

  • Intuit provides QuickBooks Online and the connection the App uses.
  • Anthropic may process a bank statement through its Claude service when the statement’s format is not recognized. Statement data sent this way is used only to read that statement.

Retention

Reconciliation working files are kept as part of ICProfit’s client records for as long as needed to provide services and meet professional record-keeping obligations. Connection tokens are deleted when a QuickBooks company is disconnected.

Your choices

A client may ask us to disconnect its QuickBooks company or delete working files at any time, and may also disconnect the App directly in QuickBooks Online.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
