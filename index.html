Why a Legal-Specific Firm

Law firm accounting is a specialty. We treat it like one.

Trust accounts, client retainers, advanced costs, and legal billing cycles are not part of a typical bookkeeper's training. A retainer recorded as revenue the day it hits the bank is a mistake that ripples through your financial statements — and your trust reporting.
